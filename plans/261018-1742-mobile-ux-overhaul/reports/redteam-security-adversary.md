# Red team: security adversary review of plan 261018-1742-mobile-ux-overhaul

Reviewer stance: an attacker looking for auth bypass, data exposure across workspaces, privilege escalation, holes in the NGAC model, stale decisions and IDOR. Each finding is checked against the codebase at `main` (36edb19).

## Finding 1: Nesting `Ch_<id>_Managers` inside `Ch_<id>_Members` means a manager can never be removed and can never leave
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Location:** Phase 07, section "Architecture" (bullet "New per-channel UA `Ch_<id>_Managers` … assigned into `Ch_<id>_Members`"); Phase 06 "Rời nhóm"
- **Flaw:** Every membership primitive in messaging works on the **direct** edge `user → Ch_<id>_Members`. Removal deletes only that edge. `requireInChannel` accepts only a user sitting directly under Members. The roster lists only direct `U` children. Once a user also reaches Members through `user → Managers → Members`, none of these primitives can see that path or cut it. The plan does not say whether a manager keeps the direct Members edge.
- **Failure scenario:** (a) The manager keeps the direct edge. When the owner removes them, or they leave, `RemoveAssignment(user, Members)` succeeds and the live socket is revoked. But `user → Managers → Members → PC` still reaches the Content OA with `{read, write, invite, manage}`, so the next `AuthorizeChannelAccess` re-admits them. They keep reading a private group they were "removed" from. (b) The manager has no direct edge. `requireInChannel` returns NotFound, so they cannot be removed or leave at all, and `ListMembers` hides them. That gives an invisible user with manage on the group.
- **Evidence:** `backend/services/messaging/internal/domain/members.go:167-171` (removes only `ChildId: target, ParentId: ch.NGACUaID`), `members.go:199-217` (`requireInChannel` uses `IsAssigned` on the direct edge), `members.go:265` (roster skips non-`U` children, so a UA child stays invisible), `members.go:181` (revokes only the socket, not the path), `channels.go:190` (Members UA sits under the workspace PC, so a nested UA reaches the PC through it).
- **Suggested fix:** Do not nest Managers under Members. Make Managers a sibling UA under the workspace PC with its own association, and make every member operation (remove, leave, demote, transfer) remove **all** of the user's edges into both UAs in one EPP-invalidated write. Add deny vectors: "manager removed from group → read DENY", "manager leaves → read DENY", and a roster test that shows managers.

## Finding 2: `manage` already means "workspace owner, moderate unseen groups", so using it as the group-manager right gives workspace owners self-promotion into private groups
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Location:** Phase 07, section "Architecture" (Managers → Content `{manage, write, invite}`; "@Tất cả = quản trị … requires `manage` on the Content OA") and "Non-functional" ("workspace owners keep `manage` on Channels OA")
- **Flaw:** Workspace Owners hold `manage` on the Channels OA, and every Content OA sits under it, so owners inherit `manage` on every group, including groups they are not in. The plan reuses `manage` as the discriminator for "group manager". It never names the operation that guards add/remove manager, settings changes or the Quản lý nhóm endpoints, so `manage` is the natural default. Every existing `manage` branch also silently becomes a group-manager power: deleting anyone's message, closing anyone's poll, removing anyone (including the Chủ nhóm), renaming, changing the avatar. Through the channel drive (an OA under Content) it also grants `manage` on the group drive.
- **Failure scenario:** A workspace owner who is not in a private HR group calls the new "make manager" endpoint with their own node id. If the endpoint checks `manage` on Content (which the owner holds by inheritance), they are assigned to Managers and gain `read`. This breaks the documented invariant "an owner reads a group only by being one of its members". Separately, the owner bypasses the @all restriction with no manager role at all. A group manager can also remove the Chủ nhóm through `RemoveMember`, which leaves `channels.owner_id` pointing at a non-member.
- **Evidence:** `backend/ngac/ngac_ops.go:48-54` (OwnerChannelOps = manage, create_channel; "an owner reads a group only by being one of its members"), `backend/services/workspace/internal/domain/service.go:248`, `backend/services/messaging/internal/domain/channels.go:186` (Content under Channels), `backend/services/drive/internal/domain/channel_drive.go:76-82` (drive OA under Content OA), `messaging/internal/domain/lifecycle.go:125` (manage = delete others' messages), `polls_tasks.go:231`, `members.go:151-160` (manage = remove anyone), `channel_avatar.go:88`, `channels.go:286`.
- **Suggested fix:** The PDP cannot tell an inherited `manage` from a direct one. So group-manager authority must be "the user is assigned to `Ch_<id>_Managers`", asked of the writer the way `requireInChannel` does, not a `manage` decision. Alternatively, keep owners' `manage` and let the spec state explicitly that it never authorizes role changes. Enumerate every existing `manage` branch in the spec with its intended holder. Add deny vectors: "workspace owner not in group → make-self-manager DENY", "manager removes owner → DENY", "workspace owner @all in restricted group → DENY".

## Finding 3: Backfilling "creator = owner" re-admits people who left or were removed
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Location:** Phase 07, "Requirements" ("Existing groups migrate with creator = owner"), "Risk Assessment" ("fall back to the earliest member"), Implementation step 3
- **Flaw:** Owner = manager, and Managers grants `{manage, write, invite}` and reaches Members. Making `channels.created_by` the owner therefore **creates an access path** for that user. Creators routinely leave their groups, and leaving deletes the graph edge but not `created_by`. The fallback source, "earliest member", must come from `channel_members`, a cache. CLAUDE.md says real membership is the assignment, and `RemoveMember` explicitly tolerates a cache row that failed to delete.
- **Failure scenario:** Alice creates "Board", leaves it, and later sensitive minutes are posted there. The migration assigns Alice to `Ch_Board_Managers`, and she can now read everything and add people. Or Bob was removed but his `channel_members` row survived a cache-delete failure. The fallback elects him owner, and he regains access. Doing this in a SQL migration also bypasses the EPP path, so replicas hold stale state until restart.
- **Evidence:** `backend/services/messaging/internal/domain/members.go:126-131` (the creator may remove people, and anyone may leave), `members.go:172-176` (a cache row may survive removal, "repeating the removal clears it"), `members.go:192-195` (creator identity comes from the business column), `data/migrations/060_private_groups.sql:87` (`created_by` used as an identity source), CLAUDE.md §3 (`channel_members` is a denormalized cache).
- **Suggested fix:** Backfill owner = `created_by` **only if** that user is currently assigned to the channel's Members UA (checked against `ngac_assignments`, not `channel_members`). Otherwise use the earliest member **present in `ngac_assignments`**. Do it in a Go backfill through the policy writer (EPP invalidation), not raw SQL. Add a vector: "creator who left → after migration read DENY".

## Finding 4: Owner is a business column, not a PDP decision, and the old creator keeps removal power after a transfer
- **Severity:** High
- **Location:** Phase 07, "Architecture" ("Owner = a manager whose user is recorded as `channels.owner_id`; the graph sees only Managers"), test vector "non-owner transfers/disbands → DENY"
- **Flaw:** The plan says "Every rule is a PDP decision", but transfer, disband and "last owner blocked" are authorized by comparing `owner_id`. The PDP cannot express them, so the promised "test vector in `services/policy/internal/ngac/`" for them cannot exist. The existing `isChannelCreator` shortcut also lets `created_by` remove anyone, regardless of the graph. The plan neither retires it nor rebinds it to `owner_id`.
- **Failure scenario:** Alice creates a group and transfers ownership to Bob. Bob demotes Alice to a plain member. Alice still passes `isChannelCreator` and removes Bob and every manager, because `RemoveMember` short-circuits on creator before any role check. `MemberRoster.CanRemove` also stays true for her.
- **Evidence:** `backend/services/messaging/internal/domain/members.go:159` (`!s.isChannelCreator(...) && !manages`), `members.go:192-195` (`ch.CreatedBy == who.UserID`), `members.go:243-244` (CanRemove uses creator).
- **Suggested fix:** Replace every `isChannelCreator` use with an `owner_id` check, or, better, model the owner as a one-member `Ch_<id>_Owner` UA so transfer and disband become graph decisions with real deny vectors. Do the transfer under a row lock with the graph write. State in the spec which rules are PEP-side, not PDP.

## Finding 5: Splitting "write" into "post vs reply with read" opens the single SendMessage gate and breaks every other write-gated action
- **Severity:** High
- **Location:** Phase 07, "Architecture" ("Members association drops `write` … thread replies authorized by the messaging service with `read` + `replies_open`"); plan.md Open question 2
- **Flaw:** `SendMessage` has one `write` check that guards every message type, including client-named `system` lines, linked approval cards and attachments. A reply is distinguished only by the client-supplied `ParentMessageID`. Relaxing to `read` when `ParentMessageID != ""` lets a read-only member post any type into a thread. Dropping `write` from Members also removes reactions, un-reacting, pins, polls, calls, editing their own messages and deleting their own messages, none of which the plan mentions. If the implementer then relaxes those to `read` "to fix the UX", the restriction is gone.
- **Failure scenario:** In an "only admins post" announcement group, a member sends `{parent_message_id: <announcement>, message_type: "system", content: "Ban quản trị: link đăng nhập mới …"}`. It passes `read` + `replies_open` and renders as a system line under the announcement. The same member cannot edit or withdraw a typo'd reply (`EditMessage` and own-delete require `write`) or react to the announcement.
- **Evidence:** `backend/services/messaging/internal/domain/messages.go:89-97` (single write check; "a 'system' line must not be a way round the right to write"; reply parent checked only after), `threads.go:24-36` (checkReplyParent), `lifecycle.go:50` (edit requires write), `lifecycle.go:125-128` (own delete = write), `reactions_pins_receipts.go:22,40,78,98` (reactions/pins = write), `polls_tasks.go:112`, `calls.go:188`.
- **Suggested fix:** Keep `write` for members and enforce "only admins post top-level" in the PEP: `ParentMessageID == ""` and the setting is on, so require Managers membership (see Finding 2). Reject `message_type != "user"` from people, and linked cards in replies. If the PDP route is kept, the spec must list every write-gated action with its intended rule, with deny tests for each.

## Finding 6: Phase 11 says "Tạo bản sao if not present", but copy is present and has no destination authorization (cross-workspace write)
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Location:** Phase 11, "Requirements" ("'Tạo bản sao' if not present"), "Architecture" (new `POST .../drive/items/:id/copy`), Implementation step 1
- **Flaw:** `POST /api/drive/items/:itemId/copy` already exists. `CopyItem` checks only `read` on the source. It never checks `write` or `upload` on the destination folder. It never checks that the destination belongs to the source's or the caller's workspace. It writes `WorkspaceID: req.DestWorkspaceId`, which the REST handler never sets, so the value is empty. The plan's own deny test ("copy without write on target") would fail today. The plan instead proposes a second, nested copy route, which leaves the vulnerable one in place.
- **Failure scenario:** Any authenticated user with read on one file posts `{target_folder_id: <folder in another tenant's drive>}`. The copy lands with `NGACNodeID = dest.NGACNodeID` and appears to every reader of that folder: content injection or phishing across tenants. The row also carries an empty `workspace_id`, which escapes workspace-scoped queries and quota. The new phone file sheet and multi-select make this one tap away.
- **Evidence:** `backend/services/drive/internal/rest/handler.go:92` (route), `handler.go:315-335` (only `target_folder_id`; no DestWorkspaceId), `backend/services/drive/internal/domain/items.go:223-268` (read on src only, line 231; dest resolved without access check, lines 236-242; `WorkspaceID: req.DestWorkspaceId`, line 262).
- **Suggested fix:** Treat this as a pre-existing authz bug for `ak:debug`/`ak:fix` before phase 11. Require `write` (and `upload`) on `dest.NGACNodeID`, set `WorkspaceID` from `dest.WorkspaceID`, refuse a destination in a different workspace than the source unless the spec allows it, and add the full deny matrix. Remove "new copy route" from phase 11 and reuse the existing route.

## Finding 7: The planned approval search route skips the approval tenancy chain, and the asset search duplicates an existing authz path
- **Severity:** High
- **Location:** Phase 08, "Architecture" (`GET /api/workspaces/:id/approvals/search`, `GET /api/workspaces/:id/assets/search`), "Related Code Files" (vite regex block)
- **Flaw:** Every approval route lives under `/api/approval`. That group runs `TenantMiddleware`, `memberMiddleware` and `tenantSchemaMiddleware`, with the tenant taken **from the JWT**. A `/api/workspaces/:id/...` route takes the tenant from the **path**. Mounted outside the group, it gets none of that middleware. If the handler then picks the per-tenant schema from `:id`, a token for tenant A searches tenant B's approval schema. The vite `/api/workspaces` dispatcher has no approval branch, so in dev the route falls through to the workspace service, and Traefik differs. Separately, asset search already exists as `GET /api/workspaces/:id/assets?search=&type_id=&state=&assigned_to=`. A parallel `/assets/search` is a second authz implementation that must duplicate the type-OA rules of `asset-authorization` and will drift.
- **Failure scenario:** A member of workspace A who knows B's id calls `/api/workspaces/B/approvals/search?q=lương`. The new handler resolves schema `tenant_B` from the path and returns B's salary requests, because no middleware compared the path to `claims.TenantID` or checked membership.
- **Evidence:** `backend/services/approval/internal/rest/handler.go:68-75` (group with Tenant, member and schema middleware), `backend/pkg/httputil/tenant.go:10-20` (tenant from claims), `backend/pkg/httputil/claims.go:17`, `frontend/vite.config.js:62-85` (dispatch branches: drive, documents, channels, contacts, asset, calendar; no approval), `backend/services/asset/internal/rest/handler.go:79,194-199` (existing search with filters).
- **Suggested fix:** Put approval search at `GET /api/approval/search` inside the existing group (tenant from the token, membership enforced), and add a deny test with a token for A plus a request naming B. Extend `ListAssets` filters instead of adding `/assets/search`. Fix the vite claim in phase 08.

## Finding 8: Star and Truy cập nhanh routes mix the path workspace with flat item ids, and "Vinh nhắc bạn" reasons leak chat data into Drive
- **Severity:** High
- **Location:** Phase 11, "Architecture" (`.../drive/starred`, `.../drive/quick-access`, `POST .../drive/items/:id/star` → "vite regex block"), "Requirements" (reason lines "Thu Lan chia sẻ", "Vinh nhắc bạn")
- **Flaw:** Drive item routes are flat (`/api/drive/items/:itemId/*`), and their workspace comes from the item. Putting star under `/api/workspaces/:id/drive/items/:itemId/star` creates two workspace sources. A star row keyed by the path `:id` lets a user plant items from workspace B into their A-scoped list. Unless the list re-derives the workspace from the item, they render under A, including in a workspace switch screenshot. The "mentions" source for Truy cập nhanh lives in messaging. Drive has no mention data, so a cross-service feed is implied but not specified. A reason line also discloses that a message mentioning you exists in a channel, and who sent it, after you lost read on that channel. The plan filters only the **item**, not the **reason**.
- **Failure scenario:** Carol is removed from #m-and-a. Her Drive home still shows "Vinh nhắc bạn" on the deal memo she can still read through a separate share. This reveals that Vinh is discussing the memo in a channel she was removed from. Or a star `POST /api/workspaces/A/drive/items/<B-item>/star` succeeds (read holds through a share), and B's item appears in A's starred list.
- **Evidence:** `backend/services/drive/internal/rest/handler.go:90-109` (flat item routes; shared-with-me flat), `handler.go:115` and `rest/sharing.go:103-128` (BatchAccess exists for re-checks), `backend/services/messaging/internal/domain/threads.go:175` (the established pattern: re-check read per recipient at read time).
- **Suggested fix:** Use flat `POST /api/drive/items/:itemId/star` with the workspace taken from the item. Have the starred and quick-access lists filter `item.workspace_id = :id`, re-check `read` per item at read time, and re-check `read` on the **source channel** for any mention or share reason (otherwise drop the reason line). Specify the cross-service contract for mentions, or cut that reason.

## Finding 9: The "who creates groups" lock is bypassed by role grants, and the lock table and graph become two sources of truth
- **Severity:** High
- **Location:** Phase 04, "Architecture" ("lock is applied by adjusting the Channels OA association (create_channel for members) through the EPP path"), "Requirements" ("PUT requires `manage` on the workspace node"; "tenant isolation by workspace id from the token")
- **Flaw:** Removing `create_channel` from `Members → Channels` does not stop anyone who holds `create_channel` through a custom role or a department. The roles editor grants exactly that op on the Channels area. Nothing stops an admin from re-granting it to a role, or a later migration or repair from re-asserting `MemberChannelOps`. Meanwhile `workspace_setting_locks` still says "locked", so the UI shows a lock the PDP does not enforce. Two more claims are wrong. "manage on the workspace node": there is no such node, the check is on the Mgmt OA, and `manage` there is held by non-owner managers too. "Workspace id from the token": the token carries `tenant_id`, and workspace-scoped routes take `:id` from the path, so the prefs and locks endpoints need `authorizeMember(caller, :id)`. Finally, `PK(user_id, coalesce(workspace_id,''), key)` is not valid Postgres, since a primary key cannot contain an expression. The likely fallback, `UNIQUE(user_id, workspace_id, key)`, treats NULLs as distinct, so account-scope rows duplicate and the read becomes nondeterministic.
- **Failure scenario:** The admin locks "Ai được tạo nhóm = Chỉ quản trị". Members in role "Trưởng nhóm" (granted `create_channel` on the Channels area last month) still create groups. The lock row says enforced, the PDP says ALLOW, and the deny test passes because it tests only the base Members UA. A user in no workspace PUTs prefs to `/api/workspaces/<any>/preferences` and gets rows written into a workspace they don't belong to.
- **Evidence:** `backend/ngac/areas.go:45-47,59-64` (roles may hold `create_channel` on Channels), `backend/services/workspace/internal/domain/roles_admin.go:280-289` (CreateAssociation for a role on an area), `workspace/internal/domain/service.go:250` (the base Members grant), `workspace/internal/domain/authz.go:12-18,52-67` (Mgmt OA, not a "workspace node"), `workspace/internal/domain/workspace_conversion.go:24-27` ("Holding manage is not enough: a manager who is not an owner"), `backend/pkg/httputil/claims.go:13-18` (token has `tenant_id`, no workspace id), `workspace/internal/domain/authz.go:70-80` (authorizeMember exists for exactly this).
- **Suggested fix:** Enforce the lock as a **prohibition** on `create_channel` over the Channels OA for Members-but-not-Owners, which deny-overrides role grants, or refuse role grants of `create_channel` while locked. Make the graph the single source and derive the lock row from it, or add a reconciliation test. Specify `authorizeMember` on every prefs and locks route, plus `authorizeAdmin(..., OpManage)` with owner-only if intended. Use a unique index on `(user_id, coalesce(workspace_id::text,''), key)` or two partial unique indexes.

## Finding 10: The @all and read-receipt locks restrict features that do not exist, so their enforcement points are unspecified
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Location:** Phase 07, "Architecture" ("@Tất cả = quản trị … messaging requires manage … to send `@all`"); Phase 04, "Architecture" ("Read receipts lock is enforced by the messaging read-marker path")
- **Flaw:** Messaging has no `@all` mention token, so there is nothing to restrict yet. The plan quietly introduces a new mass-notification feature without designing it: token format, push fan-out, and the `EditMessage` path that pushes newly added mentions. Messaging also has no "seen by" surface. Read receipts exist only as the caller's own unread markers, and `MarkChannelRead` must keep writing for unread counts to work. Enforcing the privacy option "at the read-marker path" would either break unread counts or protect nothing.
- **Failure scenario:** The implementer gates @all only in `SendMessage`. A member posts "hi", then edits it to "@all hi". `EditMessage` (write-gated, with no @all check) pushes the new mention to every member, bypassing "Ai được nhắc @Tất cả". When "Đã xem bởi" is built later, it reads `read_receipts` directly and ignores the opt-out, because the plan put enforcement on the write.
- **Evidence:** Repo-wide search for `@all`, `MentionAll` and `everyone` mention handling finds nothing in `backend/services/messaging` or `frontend/src`. `backend/services/messaging/internal/domain/lifecycle.go:41-45,70` (an edit adds mentions and pushes the new ones), `reactions_pins_receipts.go:137-163` (MarkChannelRead: the caller's own marker, read-gated, feeding unread counts), `reactions_pins_receipts.go:166-170` (unread counts come from the caller's own receipts).
- **Suggested fix:** Spec @all as a new capability (token, fan-out cap, and authorization on **both** send and edit, including replies), with deny vectors on each path. For read receipts, either drop the lock until a "seen by" surface exists, or define enforcement at the **read** of other people's markers (and any realtime read event), never at the write.

Summary: Ten findings, five of them Critical. They are: nesting Managers under Members makes removal and leaving ineffective; reusing `manage` lets workspace owners promote themselves into private groups; the creator backfill re-admits people who left; the existing drive copy route has no destination authorization and writes across workspaces, which phase 11 assumes is absent.
