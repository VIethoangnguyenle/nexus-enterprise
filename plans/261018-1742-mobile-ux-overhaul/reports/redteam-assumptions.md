# Red team: assumption destroyer and scope audit

Plan: `plans/261018-1742-mobile-ux-overhaul/` (plan.md and phases 01–12). Reviewed 2026-10-19 against `main` at 36edb19.
Every claim below was checked with grep against the worktree.

## Finding 1: Taking `write` away from Members also takes away reactions, pins, polls, tasks, calls, editing and thread replies
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Location:** phase-07 Architecture, line 29 ("Members association drops `write` → `{read, invite?}`; thread replies authorized by the messaging service with `read` + `replies_open`"); Implementation step 2 (test vectors).
- **Flaw:** The plan assumes `write` on the Content OA means "post a top-level message". In the code it gates almost every member action in a group. The plan also assumes thread replies can be split out with a `read` check. But `SendMessage` checks `write` before it looks at `ParentMessageID` at all.
- **Failure scenario:** An owner sets "Ai được gửi tin = Chỉ chủ nhóm và quản trị nhóm". Members then cannot react, pin or unpin, create or vote in polls, create tasks, start or join a call, or edit or back out their own messages. Thread replies fail too, even though Open question 2 and the test vector "member replies in thread → ALLOW" say they work. The phase 07 test matrix only covers post, reply, invite and @all, so all of this ships without a test catching it.
- **Evidence:** `backend/services/messaging/internal/domain/messages.go:92` (`authorizeChannelOp(... ngac.OpWrite)` before `if in.ParentMessageID != ""` at :95); `reactions_pins_receipts.go:22` (AddReaction), `:78` (PinMessage), `:98` (UnpinMessage); `polls_tasks.go:112`, `:280`, `:413`; `calls.go:188`, `:263`, `:559`; `lifecycle.go:50` (EditMessage), `:127` (DeleteMessage back-out); `backend/ngac/ngac_ops.go:62-64` (`ChannelMemberOps` = read, write, invite).
- **Suggested fix:** Keep `write` on Members. Treat "who may post" as a messaging-side rule checked only in `SendMessage` for top-level posts, keyed on the channel setting plus `manage`. This is the same approach the plan already uses for @all. If the NGAC route is kept, the spec has to list every `OpWrite` call site with its intended allow/deny under restriction, and the deny matrix in step 5 has to cover each one.

## Finding 2: A `Ch_<id>_Managers` UA under `Ch_<id>_Members` breaks the membership rule the code and migration 061 enforce
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Location:** phase-07 Architecture, line 26 ("New per-channel UA `Ch_<id>_Managers` … assigned into `Ch_<id>_Members`").
- **Flaw:** Today a person is the only kind of node allowed directly under a group's Members UA. The roster, remove-member and in-channel checks all read direct `U` children only. A reconcile migration already deletes any UA it finds there.
- **Failure scenario:** Suppose a manager is moved from Members into Managers. They drop out of `ListMembers`. `requireInChannel` then fails for them, so they cannot be removed or demoted, and the channel_members cache drifts. Suppose instead they are double-assigned. Then demotion has to edit two edges without a transaction. Separately, if 061's reconcile logic is re-run or copied (it is written to be re-run), it deletes every Managers UA edge, and every group silently loses its managers.
- **Evidence:** `backend/services/messaging/internal/domain/members.go:246-266` (`ListMembers`: "user nodes directly under its Members UA", `if n.NodeType != ngac.TypeU { continue }`); `members.go:197-199` (`requireInChannel`: "a person sitting directly under the channel's Members UA"); `members.go:135-136` ("The target must be a person directly in the group, not a UA"); `data/migrations/061_channel_membership_reconcile.sql` header item b) "any UA assigned under a non-DM group's Members UA (a person is the only kind of node a group holds)" and its pre-flight `n.node_type = 'UA'` (line ~46).
- **Suggested fix:** Phase 01's spec has to decide the topology explicitly. One option: Managers is a sibling UA with its own association to the Content OA, and managers keep their direct Members edge. Another option: amend the 061 invariant and the three membership functions in the same commit. Add test vectors for roster, remove and demote.

## Finding 3: The "owner" already exists as `channels.created_by`, and the plan adds a second one plus a phase-order bug
- **Severity:** High
- **Location:** phase-07 lines 28 and 57 (`channels.owner_id`; "Backfill … old groups without a creator → fall back to the earliest member"); phase-06 lines 20 and 40 ("last owner blocked").
- **Flaw:** `created_by` already exists, and `isChannelCreator` already grants removal rights from it. The plan adds `owner_id` without saying what happens to `created_by`'s privileges. Separately, phase 06 depends only on 02 but requires a "last owner" check, and owners only exist after phase 07.
- **Failure scenario:** After a transfer, the original creator can still remove anyone through `isChannelCreator`, while the new owner holds the Managers grant. Two people can now act as owner, and the "exactly one owner" requirement fails. Phase 06's success criterion "the last owner is blocked" cannot pass, because nothing defines an owner yet.
- **Evidence:** `data/init.sql:96` (`created_by TEXT REFERENCES users(id)`); `backend/services/messaging/internal/domain/members.go:159` (`!s.isChannelCreator(...)` grants removal) and `:192-195` (`ch.CreatedBy == who.UserID`); `data/migrations/060_private_groups.sql:87` already relies on `created_by`.
- **Suggested fix:** Reuse `created_by` as the owner, or rename it in the migration. Then route `isChannelCreator` through the owner, so transfer moves the removal right too. Move "last owner blocked" into phase 07, or make phase 06 depend on 07.

## Finding 4: The "Ai được tạo nhóm" lock on the Members→Channels association can be bypassed, and it creates a second source of truth
- **Severity:** High
- **Location:** phase-04 Architecture, lines 29-31 ("applied by adjusting the Channels OA association (create_channel for members) through the EPP path"); Implementation step 2.
- **Flaw:** `create_channel` on the Channels OA does not come only from the workspace Members UA. Custom roles can be granted it through the existing permission-areas admin screen, and Owners hold it too. The lock row in `workspace_setting_locks` and the graph association can disagree, and nothing reconciles them. `SetRolePermissions` does not know the lock exists.
- **Failure scenario:** An admin sets "only admins create groups". A member who also holds the custom role "Trưởng nhóm", which has the Channels area enabled, can still create groups. Or an admin re-enables `create_channel` for a role on the roles screen while the lock says it is off. The step-2 test passes because it only exercises plain Members, and the lock leaks in production. Workspace provisioning also writes the Members association unconditionally, so any re-provision or repair path silently undoes the lock.
- **Evidence:** `backend/ngac/areas.go:60-64` (`AreaChannels` → `{OpCreateChannel}` grantable to roles); `backend/services/workspace/internal/rest/admin_people_handler.go:43` (`PUT /workspaces/:id/roles/:roleId/permissions/:area`); `backend/services/workspace/internal/domain/roles_admin.go:224`; `backend/ngac/ngac_ops.go:52-54` (`OwnerChannelOps` includes create_channel); `backend/services/workspace/internal/domain/service.go:250` (provisioning writes `membersUA → channelsOA, MemberChannelOps()`); `backend/services/messaging/internal/domain/channels.go:84` (the only check is `create_channel` on the Channels OA, from any source).
- **Suggested fix:** Pick one mechanism. One option: a prohibition, which already exists as deny-overrides (`017_ngac_prohibitions.sql`), on `create_channel` for the workspace Members UA with an Owners/admin exemption. Another option: the lock is the existing Members-association toggle itself, and the role area UI refuses to grant `create_channel` while it is locked. Add deny vectors for role-holder, department-holder and post-repair cases.

## Finding 5: Phase 04 puts preferences in auth and workspace, but the existing stores belong to messaging and calendar, and nothing connects them
- **Severity:** High
- **Location:** phase-04 Requirements, Non-functional lines 22-23 ("the registry maps their keys to them instead of duplicating"); Architecture lines 26-27 ("Auth service owns account-scope prefs … workspace service owns workspace-scope prefs and locks"; "Read receipts lock is enforced by the messaging read-marker path"); Related Code Files; effort 1.5d.
- **Flaw:** `push_preferences` and `channel_notification_levels` are owned by messaging, and `calendar_user_settings` is owned by calendar. A registry in auth or workspace cannot "map keys" to them without either cross-service SQL, which the layering rules forbid, or new gRPC RPCs. Messaging has no workspace client at all, so the read-receipt lock and the `chat.readReceipts` preference cannot be read on the read-marker path. The keys also differ: `calendar_user_settings` uses `user_node_id`, while the proposed `user_preferences` uses `user_id`.
- **Failure scenario:** One of two outcomes. The implementer duplicates `notify.*` and `calendar.*` into `user_preferences`, which breaks the "no key stored twice" test and the "one source per option" decision. Or the implementer adds workspace→messaging, messaging→workspace and auth→messaging gRPC, which needs a `.proto` change, `make proto`, `npm run proto:gen`, and new Traefik/vite routes, none of it scoped. Either way 1.5d is not realistic, and phases 02, 05 and 10, which all depend on 04, slip.
- **Evidence:** `backend/services/messaging/internal/store/push.go` (only Go owner of `push_preferences`); `frontend/vite.config.js:50` and `docker-compose.yml:366` (`/api/me/push-preferences` → messaging); `data/migrations/057_calendar.sql:125-132` (`calendar_user_settings` keyed by `user_node_id`; used in `backend/services/calendar/internal/store/events.go`); `backend/services/messaging/cmd/main.go:68-82` (messaging dials policy, auth and drive only, no workspace client); `backend/proto/workspace/workspace.proto` has no preference or lock RPC.
- **Suggested fix:** Give each preference to the service that enforces it: chat, read-receipt and notify keys to messaging; calendar keys to calendar; drive keys to drive; `mobile.bar` to workspace. The client reads from several endpoints, as phase 08 already does. Lock enforcement for read receipts then lives in messaging next to its data. List every proto change and both regenerations in phase 04, and re-estimate the effort.

## Finding 6: The proposed `user_preferences` DDL is invalid in Postgres
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Location:** phase-04 Architecture, line 28 (`user_preferences(user_id, workspace_id NULL, key, value jsonb, updated_at, PK(user_id, coalesce(workspace_id,''), key))`).
- **Flaw:** A PRIMARY KEY cannot contain an expression, and every PK column is implicitly NOT NULL. That contradicts `workspace_id NULL` for account-scope rows. `workspace_setting_locks` has no stated key or FK, and `locked_by` has no type (user_id or node id?), even though the 409 response must show the lock owner's display name.
- **Failure scenario:** The migration fails on `make db-migrate`. A quick fix such as a sentinel `''` workspace_id then breaks a `REFERENCES workspaces(id) ON DELETE CASCADE` FK, so either preferences leak after a workspace is deleted or the FK is dropped.
- **Evidence:** plan text itself (phase-04 line 28); compare the repo's own pattern of separate scoped tables keyed by real FKs: `data/migrations/041_chat_sections.sql` (`workspace_id … REFERENCES workspaces(id) ON DELETE CASCADE`, unique index on an expression), `043_push_preferences.sql:6`.
- **Suggested fix:** Use two tables (account-scope and workspace-scope), or use a surrogate PK plus `CREATE UNIQUE INDEX … (user_id, COALESCE(workspace_id,''), key)` with a nullable FK. Specify the `workspace_setting_locks` PK `(workspace_id, key)`, the FK and the `locked_by` type.

## Finding 7: The approval search route is on a path neither Traefik nor the approval service serves
- **Severity:** High
- **Location:** phase-08 Architecture, line 27 (`GET /api/workspaces/:id/approvals/search`); Success Criteria "Routes in vite proxy regex block; work in native dev".
- **Flaw:** The approval service mounts only `/api/approval/*`, behind `TenantMiddleware` and a per-tenant schema middleware. It has no `/workspaces/:id` routes. In Docker and production, Traefik sends `/api/workspaces/*` to the workspace service and sends approval traffic only on `/api/approval`. The plan's constraint mentions only `vite.config.js`, so the Traefik labels in `docker-compose.yml` and the deploy compose are out of scope.
- **Failure scenario:** The route works in native dev after a vite regex branch is added. In production it hits the workspace service and returns 404, so the Đề nghị group in unified search is always empty. The 390px e2e run in native dev does not catch this.
- **Evidence:** `backend/services/approval/internal/rest/handler.go:68-76` (`api := e.Group("/api", … TenantMiddleware(), … tenantSchemaMiddleware())`, `approval := api.Group("/approval")`); `docker-compose.yml:476` (approval router `PathPrefix(/api/approval)` only), `docker-compose.yml:285` (workspace router `PathPrefix(/api/workspaces)`); `frontend/vite.config.js:55-88` (regex block has no approval branch).
- **Suggested fix:** Use `GET /api/approval/search` (tenant-scoped like every other approval route), which needs no proxy or Traefik change. Otherwise, add the Traefik label to both compose files and list them in Related Code Files.

## Finding 9: Drag-to-dismiss the keyboard is not possible on iOS web, and the visualViewport code the plan reuses is a boolean, not a tracker
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Location:** phase-10 line 21 ("keyboard dismiss follows the finger"); line 28 ("keyboard tracking uses `visualViewport` (already used for the docked composer)"); Success Criteria "Every row of the §9 table implemented".
- **Flaw:** iOS Safari and standalone PWAs give web content no way to dismiss the keyboard interactively. A page can only `blur()`, and the keyboard then animates down on its own. The existing hook only reports whether the keyboard is open, on `resize`, which on iOS fires after the animation ends.
- **Failure scenario:** The implementer cannot make the keyboard follow the finger. The phase 10 criterion "every row of §9" and phase 12's device checklist then fail on that row by construction, which pushes the work back into an `ak:debug` → `ak:fix` loop with no fix available.
- **Evidence:** `frontend/src/hooks/useKeyboardOpen.ts:10-24` (subscribes to `resize`; `snapshot()` returns a boolean, `innerHeight - vv.height > KEYBOARD_MIN_PX`); `frontend/src/lib/app-height.ts:1`, `frontend/src/main.tsx:37` (height tracking only).
- **Suggested fix:** Redefine the §9 row as "dragging the stream down past N px blurs the composer (keyboard closes)". Update the mockup and the spec in phase 01 so the acceptance line can actually be met.

## Finding 10: The client hides actions using a permission cache that stays stale for 30 seconds after a role change
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Location:** phase-06 Non-functional line 25 ("every action hidden when the PDP would refuse it (`useObjectPermissions`)"); phase-07 Non-functional "decisions stay fresh (EPP invalidation on every change)"; Success Criteria "UI hides Quản lý nhóm for non-managers".
- **Flaw:** EPP invalidation makes the server fresh, but the client is not. `useObjectPermissions` caches each object's answer for 30s through `/drive/batch-access`, and the plan adds no permission event for role or setting changes.
- **Failure scenario:** A manager is demoted, or posting is restricted. For up to 30s the person still sees Quản lý nhóm, invite and the composer, and every tap gets a server refusal. The plan defines no copy or error path for a mid-session demotion, and its no-ID rule means a raw 403 cannot be shown either.
- **Evidence:** `frontend/src/hooks/usePermissions.ts:74` (`staleTime: 30_000`); `frontend/src/api/access.ts:40` (`/drive/batch-access`); `frontend/src/hooks/useCanManageChannel.ts:12-20` (the existing pattern for channel OA perms).
- **Suggested fix:** Phase 07 has messaging publish a realtime event on role or setting change that invalidates `keys.permissions.object(tenantId, contentOA)`. It also defines the refused-action copy for each new decision, and adds an e2e test for demotion during a session.

Status: DONE
Summary: Ten load-bearing assumptions fail against the repo. Two are Critical: removing Members `write` cascades into reactions, pins, polls, calls, edits and thread replies, and a Managers UA under Members contradicts ListMembers, requireInChannel and migration 061. Four are High: the duplicate owner column (with the phase-06 dependency on owners that only exist in 07), the bypassable group-creation lock, preference ownership split across services with no connection, and the approval search route production does not serve. The rest are invalid DDL, false reuse claims, iOS keyboard dismissal, and the 30s client permission cache.
